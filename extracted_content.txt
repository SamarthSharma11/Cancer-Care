Compassion That Becomes Action

Cancer is not only a medical challenge; it can also create significant emotional and financial pressure for patients and their families. Cancer Care & Cure Foundation is being developed with the aim of helping financially disadvantaged cancer patients access appropriate support, information, and resources.

Our goal is to bring together individuals, corporate partners, healthcare institutions, and philanthropic organisations to help reduce financial barriers to cancer care. Any assistance will depend on programme availability, eligibility, verified need, available funds, and applicable law.

Our vision

A society where financial hardship does not prevent people affected by cancer from seeking essential medical care.

How we hope to help

Consider financial assistance for eligible cancer-related expenses when a funded programme is available.

Help patients and caregivers find reliable information about hospitals, government schemes, and charitable resources.

Promote cancer awareness and timely consultation with qualified healthcare professionals.

Build responsible partnerships with donors, companies, hospitals, and community organisations.

2. ABOUT US

Who we are

Cancer Care & Cure Foundation is being developed to help people affected by cancer face their journey with greater support, dignity, and hope. We aspire to work with patients and caregivers, healthcare institutions, community organisations, corporate partners, donors, and volunteers.

The nature and scale of our activities will depend on the foundation’s legal status, available resources, partnerships, and approved programme policies.

Our mission

To build a responsible, transparent, and compassionate support network that helps eligible cancer patients and their families access information, assistance, awareness initiatives, and appropriate support services.

Our objectives

Work towards financial assistance for eligible cancer-treatment expenses, subject to available funds and programme rules.

Support access to information about cancer surgery, chemotherapy, radiation therapy, prescribed medicines, and diagnostic investigations.

Collaborate with credible hospitals, cancer centres, healthcare professionals, and charitable institutions.

Explore corporate and philanthropic partnerships where legal and eligibility requirements are met.

Promote awareness, early detection education, and timely medical consultation.

Maintain accountability, protect privacy, and use resources responsibly.

Our values

Compassion: Treating patients and families with empathy and respect.

Dignity: Protecting privacy and avoiding stigma or discrimination.

Integrity: Handling contributions and enquiries responsibly.

Transparency: Communicating eligibility, limitations, and fund use clearly.

Collaboration: Working with credible partners to improve reach and impact.

Hope: Encouraging patients and caregivers without making unrealistic promises.

Message from the Founder

Sushil Sharma

Founder, Cancer Care & Cure Foundation

My vision for Cancer Care & Cure Foundation comes from a deeply personal journey. In 2019, I was diagnosed with pancreatic cancer. My journey through major surgery, chemotherapy, radiation therapy, and recovery taught me that facing cancer requires not only medical treatment but also courage, emotional strength, family support, and financial resources.

I experienced how physically and emotionally challenging cancer can be, and how the financial burden can make an already difficult situation even more overwhelming. My experience strengthened my desire to help people who may have the courage to fight cancer but lack the financial means to access the care they need.

Through this initiative, I hope to bring together corporate organisations, CSR partners, individual donors, philanthropic institutions, and compassionate people who share the belief that every life matters. The aim is to help ensure that financial hardship does not become an unnecessary barrier to seeking essential medical care.

“Cancer can touch your body, but not your soul.”

Our commitment: We aim to handle enquiries respectfully, assess requests fairly, safeguard personal information, and develop programmes only when appropriate systems and resources are in place.

3. OUR PROGRAMMES

The following are proposed programme areas. They should be presented as planned or developing until formally approved, funded, and launched.

A. Patient assistance

We aim to develop a process for considering requests from cancer patients experiencing financial hardship. Depending on available funds, verified need, eligibility criteria, and programme rules, support may include assistance towards approved treatment-related costs or referral to credible support resources. Assistance cannot be guaranteed.

B. Treatment navigation and information

We aim to help patients and caregivers find reliable information about treatment pathways, hospital support desks, government schemes, charitable assistance, and relevant resources. The foundation will not provide diagnosis or replace medical professionals.

C. Awareness and early-detection education

We aim to support educational activities that encourage people to discuss symptoms, risk factors, screening options, and timely medical evaluation with qualified healthcare professionals.

D. Emotional support and caregiver awareness

Where resources and suitable partners are available, we aim to connect patients and caregivers with appropriate support groups, counselling resources, and community networks.

E. Community and volunteer engagement

We aim to involve volunteers and community partners in awareness, fundraising, outreach, administration, and other activities suited to their skills and the foundation’s safeguarding policies.

Programme safeguards

Programmes will depend on available resources and written eligibility criteria.

Requests may require supporting identity, medical, income, or cost-estimate documents, handled with appropriate privacy safeguards.

A donation or enquiry does not guarantee assistance, treatment outcomes, or preferential access to medical services.

The foundation will not promise medical outcomes or replace advice from qualified clinicians.

6. PATIENT ASSISTANCE

Looking for support?

A cancer diagnosis can bring difficult medical, emotional, and financial questions. Cancer Care & Cure Foundation aims to develop a respectful process for people seeking information or assistance.

How to contact us

Email cccf.cancer@gmail.com or call +91 9810414100.

Briefly explain the type of support or information you are seeking. Do not send highly sensitive documents until a suitable and secure submission method has been confirmed.

If a support programme is available, the team will explain its eligibility requirements, documentation, process, and limitations.

Requests may be reviewed based on programme rules, verified need, available funds, and applicable requirements.

Important information

Submitting an enquiry does not guarantee financial assistance or another service.

Support depends on programme availability, eligibility, verification, and available resources.

The foundation does not provide emergency medical services, diagnosis, or treatment.

For urgent symptoms or a medical emergency, contact your treating hospital or local emergency services immediately.

Do not email full medical records, identity documents, bank details, or other sensitive information unless requested through an appropriate process.
